# IFC FY24 Net Income and Total Assets Report
## IFC FY24 Net Income
During the fiscal year ended June 30, 2024 (FY24), the International Finance Corporation (IFC) recorded a net income of $1.5 billion (specifically $1,485 million), representing a significant increase of $813 million compared to the net income of $672 million reported in FY23 (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 5, 44).

This positive financial performance was primarily driven by the following factors (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 5, 44):
* **Higher Treasury Income:** Net treasury income grew by $619 million compared to FY23.
* **Strong Net Interest Income on Loans and Debt Securities:** Total income from loans and debt securities (net of allocated charges on borrowings) increased by $298 million.
* **Equity and Investment Gains:** Equity income increased by $8 million, and there was an increase of $48 million in unrealized gains on equity investments. Unrealized gains on loans and debt securities also saw a modest increase of $7 million.

These positive drivers were partially offset by several negative year-over-year variances, including (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 44):
* A $97 million decrease in unrealized gains on borrowings.
* A $90 million increase in loss provisions (IFC recorded a net provision for losses on loans, available-for-sale debt securities, off-balance sheet credit exposures, and other receivables of $21 million in FY24 compared to a provision of $29 million in FY23, but experienced related adjustments that negatively impacted the net income comparison).
* A modest $4 million increase in administrative expenses and pensions.

Overall, the strong treasury performance and interest income environment led to a robust net income of $1,485 million for the year (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 44).
