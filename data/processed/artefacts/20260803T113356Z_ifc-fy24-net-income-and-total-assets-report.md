# IFC FY24 Net Income and Total Assets Report
## IFC FY24 Net Income
For the fiscal year ended June 30, 2024 (FY24), the International Finance Corporation (IFC) recorded a net income of **$1,485 million** (or approximately **$1.5 billion**). This represents a substantial increase of $813 million compared to the net income of **$672 million** reported in FY23 (IFC 2024 Annual Report Financial Statements, p. 5, 44).

According to IFC's financial statements, the primary drivers of this strong financial performance in FY24 were:
*   **Higher Treasury Income:** Strong performance across treasury operations.
*   **Strong Net Interest Income:** Improved yields and margins on loans and debt securities (IFC 2024 Annual Report Financial Statements, p. 5).

Additionally, Income Available for Designations grew to $1.6 billion in FY24, compared to $681 million in FY23 (IFC 2024 Annual Report Financial Statements, p. 5).
