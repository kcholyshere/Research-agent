# IFC FY24 Financial Performance Report
## IFC FY24 Net Income
In the fiscal year ended June 30, 2024 (FY24), the International Finance Corporation (IFC) reported a consolidated net income of **$1,485 million** [IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 44, 64]. This represents a significant increase of **$813 million** (or approximately 121%) compared to the net income of **$672 million** reported in FY23, and a strong recovery from the net loss of **$464 million** recorded in FY22 [IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 44, 64].

### Key Drivers of Net Income Growth
The improvement in net income was primarily driven by the following factors:
* **Income from Investments:** Total investment income rose to **$4,136 million** in FY24 from **$2,970 million** in FY23 [IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 64]. This includes:
  * **Loans and Guarantees:** Income rose to **$3,204 million** (up from $2,290 million in FY23) [IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 64].
  * **Debt Securities:** Income increased to **$811 million** (up from $518 million in FY23) [IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 64].
* **Liquid Asset Trading Activities:** Income from liquid asset trading activities surged to **$2,391 million** in FY24, compared to **$1,464 million** in FY23, reflecting favorable market conditions and Treasury performance [IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 64].
* **Lower Loss Provisions:** Provisions for losses on loans, off-balance sheet credit exposures, and other receivables decreased to **$9 million** in FY24 from **$22 million** in FY23 [IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 64].

### Offsetting Expenses
These positive impacts were partially offset by:
* **Charges on Borrowings:** Interest and other charges on borrowings increased substantially to **$3,815 million** in FY24 from **$2,598 million** in FY23, driven by higher market interest rates [IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 64].
* **Administrative and Advisory Expenses:** Total other expenses rose to **$1,827 million** in FY24 from **$1,721 million** in FY23, with administrative expenses increasing from $1,430 million to $1,516 million [IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 64].
