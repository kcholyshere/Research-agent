# IFC FY24 Net Income and Total Assets Report
## FY24 Net Income
For the fiscal year ended June 30, 2024 (FY24), the International Finance Corporation (IFC) recorded a consolidated net income of **$1,485 million**. This represents a significant increase of $813 million (or approximately 121%) compared to the net income of **$672 million** reported in FY23. In comparison, IFC had recorded a net loss of **$464 million** in FY22 (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, Page 64, 65).

### Key Drivers of FY24 Net Income
The growth in net income was primarily driven by strong investment performance and trading gains, offset by higher borrowing costs:
* **Income from Investments and Liquid Assets:** Total income from investments rose to **$4,136 million** in FY24 from $2,970 million in FY23. This was driven by a substantial increase in income from loans and guarantees ($3,204 million in FY24 vs. $2,290 million in FY23) and income from debt securities ($811 million in FY24 vs. $518 million in FY23) (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, Page 64).
* **Liquid Asset Trading Activities:** Income from liquid asset trading activities increased significantly to **$2,391 million** in FY24, up from $1,464 million in FY23 (and a net loss of $413 million in FY22) (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, Page 64).
* **Borrowing Charges:** These gains were partially offset by a rise in charges on borrowings, which increased to **$3,815 million** in FY24 from $2,598 million in FY23, reflecting the elevated interest rate environment (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, Page 64).
* **Net Income after Borrowing Charges:** Consequently, income from investments and liquid asset trading activities, after charges on borrowings, grew to **$2,712 million** in FY24 from $1,836 million in FY23 (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, Page 64).
