# IFC FY24 Financial Performance: Net Income and Total Assets
## IFC FY24 Net Income
IFC's net income for the fiscal year ended June 30, 2024 (FY24) was **$1,485 million** (approximately $1.5 billion) (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 44). This represents a substantial increase of $813 million from the net income of **$672 million** reported in FY23 (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 5, 44).

According to management's discussion, the performance increase was primarily driven by:
* **Higher Treasury Income**: Strong yields on the liquid asset portfolio.
* **Net Interest Income**: High interest income on loans and debt securities (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 5).

Additionally, IFC reported that Income Available for Designations reached **$1.6 billion** in FY24, compared with $681 million in FY23 (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, p. 5).
