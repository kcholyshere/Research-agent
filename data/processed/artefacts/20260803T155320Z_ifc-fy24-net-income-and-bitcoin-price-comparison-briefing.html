IFC FY24 Net Income Analysis

According to the IFC's consolidated financial statements for the fiscal year ended June 30, 2024, the corporation achieved a net income of $1,485 million (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, Page 64). The growth in net income was mainly driven by robust treasury performance and strong net interest income from loans and debt securities. Net income has recovered sharply from FY22 when the IFC experienced a net loss of $464 million (IFC Annual Report 2024 Financials, Page 64).
