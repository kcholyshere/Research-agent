## IFC FY24 Net Income Overview

This represents a significant increase of $813 million (or 121%) compared to the net income of $672 million recorded in FY23 [ifc-annual-report-2024-financials.pdf, Page 44]. For context, IFC had recorded a net loss of $464 million in FY22 [ifc-annual-report-2024-financials.pdf, Page 44].

The strong financial performance in FY24 was primarily driven by higher treasury income and robust net interest income on loans and debt securities:
* **Net Treasury Income:** Provided the largest positive variance, driving a $619 million increase in net income compared to FY23 [ifc-annual-report-2024-financials.pdf, Page 44].
* **Income from Loans and Debt Securities:** Contributed an additional $298 million to the year-over-year increase, net of allocated charges on borrowings [ifc-annual-report-2024-financials.pdf, Page 44].

These positive drivers were partially offset by an increase in administrative and pension expenses (which reduced net income by $90 million) and higher provisions for losses (which reduced net income by $97 million) [ifc-annual-report-2024-financials.pdf, Page 44]. 

Furthermore, IFC's Income Available for Designations reached $1.6 billion in FY24, compared to $681 million in FY23 [ifc-annual-report-2024-financials.pdf, Page 5]. Starting in FY24, this designation measure excludes income from the Post-retirement Contribution Reserve Fund (PCRF) [ifc-annual-report-2024-financials.pdf, Page 5].
